enforcement tiers

Three tiers. Stated plainly, not blended.

Most governance tools imply one guarantee and quietly deliver another. Cyraduct names three distinct guarantees so a buyer knows exactly what they're deploying — and what they're not.

1
Advisory
where it sits

Outside the execution path. Cyraduct returns a decision; nothing physically stops the action.

guarantees

A documented, timestamped decision trail for every evaluated action.

does not guarantee

That a flagged action is actually prevented if the caller ignores the response.

2
Attested
where it sits

Outside the path — but the destination system refuses to act without a valid receipt.

guarantees

No compliant sink executes without a current, unexpired, unrevoked receipt.

does not guarantee

Protection if a sink chooses not to enforce the receipt requirement, or is bypassed entirely.

3
Broker-enforced
where it sits

Inside the execution path, as a gateway or sidecar. Nothing reaches the destination unchecked.

guarantees

No bypass path exists at the network layer for integrated actions.

does not guarantee

Zero operational cost — availability and latency now depend on Cyraduct itself.

non-goals

What Cyraduct will not claim.

A governance protocol earns trust by being honest about its limits before someone else finds them. This list is published, versioned, and load-bearing — not a disclaimer buried in a footer.

—

Does not prevent all misuse. It reduces the surface for unauthorized or unreviewed consequential actions. It cannot guarantee the absence of failure, evasion, or novel attack paths.

—

Does not replace identity or access management. Cyraduct consumes identity signals from your existing IAM/PAM stack; it does not issue or own identity.

—

Does not adjudicate legal liability. It produces evidence for legal, compliance, and regulatory review — it does not determine fault or compliance status itself.

—

Does not author domain policy unilaterally. Policy packs are versioned and attributed to a named signing authority — Cyraduct hosts and enforces them, it doesn't write your industry's rules for you.

—

Does not require full custody to provide value. Attested mode gives enforceable guarantees without making Cyraduct a routing bottleneck for every action.
